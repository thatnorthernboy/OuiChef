FAQ

Common questions.

Does ReviewAI post replies automatically without me seeing them?
Only if you enable auto-post mode. By default, every AI-generated reply is sent to you for approval first. You can review, edit, or reject it before anything goes live. You're always in control.
Will the replies sound like a robot?
No. ReviewAI generates replies based on your restaurant's name, tone settings, and the specific content of each review. The result sounds human, empathetic, and specific — not a copy-paste template. You can always personalize further before posting.
What platforms do you support?
We currently support Google Business reviews. Yelp and TripAdvisor integrations are coming in Q3 2025. Most restaurant owners find that Google covers 70–80% of their review volume.
Do I need a credit card to start the free trial?
No. You get 30 days completely free with no credit card required. At the end of the trial, you choose a plan or cancel — no questions asked.
Can I manage multiple restaurant locations?
Yes. The Pro plan supports up to 5 locations under one dashboard. The Enterprise plan supports unlimited locations, ideal for groups and franchises.
